## 4. FACE CHARACTER

### 4.1 Face Type

**Natural East Asian Female** — NOT a celebrity template face.

- **Face Shape:** Soft oval to elliptical
- **Geometry:** Clear but gentle contours (not overly sharp, not amorphous)
- **Skin Tone:** Natural, warm undertone; accepts mild variation across regions
- **Texture:** Real, with subtle natural surface — NOT plastic-smooth / over-blurred

### 4.2 Key Facial Features

#### Eyes
- **Shape:** Calm, focused, attentive gaze
- **Expression:** Listening quality, not staring
- **Blink:** Natural frequency (not robotic, not exaggerated)
- **Look:** Steady without intensity

#### Eyebrows
- **Shape:** Natural, not heavily sculpted
- **Arch:** Soft arch, expressive without being theatrical
- **Symmetry:** Slightly asymmetrical (human)

#### Nose
- **Type:** Natural bridge, proportional to face
- **Finish:** No emphasis on cosmetic perfection

#### Mouth
- **Size:** Proportional to lower face
- **Smile:** Reserved, genuine—not a permanent expression
- **Texture:** Matte or satin finish (not glossy/promotional)
- **Color:** Natural lip tone matching warm face

#### Overall
- **Skin Quality:** Allows subtle texture, light freckles, natural variations
- **No:** Overmorphing / extreme contour / plastic appearance
- **No:** Celebrity lookalike design
- **No:** Anime/cartoon stylization
- **Yes:** Warmth + Intelligence + Calm

## 5. HAIR

### 5.1 Primary Signature Hairstyle

**Length:** Mid-length (shoulder to mid-back)  
**Color:** Deep brown / dark brown with warm undertone  
**Texture:** Natural waves with soft layering (not aggressively curled, not poker-straight)  
**Volume:** Moderate, living volume (not flat, not heavily styled)

### 5.2 Signature Recognition

The hair must be distinctive enough for users to recognize the character at a distance.

- Signature swept volume on one side
- Subtle natural highlights suggesting warmth
- Occasional loose strand (human, not perfect)

### 5.3 Alternate Hairstyles (Future, Post V1)

- Same length, slightly different part
- Same color, different styling (professional updo, casual tie-back)

**Status in V1:** Primary signature only.

## 7. WARDROBE

### 7.1 Design Principle

Wardrobe signals **accessibility + credibility + warmth**, never authority, sales, or formality.

### 7.2 Three Signature Looks

#### **LOOK_A: COMPANION** (陪伴)
- **Intent:** Family conversation, household counseling setting
- **Garment:** Soft sweater or light knit + neutral undershirt
- **Color:** Warm neutrals (cream, light gray, soft camel, pale blue)
- **Fit:** Relaxed, comfortable, not tight
- **Accessories:** Minimal (optional simple earrings)
- **Feeling:** "Come, sit, let's talk"

#### **LOOK_B: PRINCIPAL** (讲解)
- **Intent:** Explaining family education concepts, teaching moment
- **Garment:** Structured but soft blazer + simple undershirt
- **Color:** Navy, charcoal, or deep olive
- **Fit:** Tailored but not corporate—suggests knowledge without authority
- **Accessories:** Minimal jewelry (pendant, simple ring)
- **Feeling:** "Here's what I've understood; let me share"

#### **LOOK_C: WARM EVENING** (温暖夜晚)
- **Intent:** "今晚怎么说" (How to say it tonight) sessions
- **Garment:** Warm-toned knit or sweater
- **Color:** Burnt orange, warm terracotta, deep rust, soft rose
- **Fit:** Cozy, not formal
- **Accessories:** Minimal, warm
- **Feeling:** "We're in this together, tonight"

### 7.3 Prohibited Wardrobe

❌ Livestream host outfit  
❌ Uniform / schoolteacher formal  
❌ Luxury/ostentatious branding  
❌ Business/sales formal wear  
❌ Childlike / baby-doll clothing  
❌ Heavily accessorized glamorous

## 12. CAMERA & LIGHTING

### 12.1 Camera Framing

**Default Framing:** Mid-chest to top of head  
**Distance:** Conversational (not extreme close-up, not distant)  
**Angle:** Slightly above neutral (viewer at ease, not looking down at avatar)  

### 12.2 Lighting Design

**Principle:** Warm, even, human

- **Key Light:** Soft, 45° angle, warm color temperature (3500K)
- **Fill Light:** Soft opposite side, 2–3 stops down
- **Back Light:** Subtle rim to separate from background
- **Shadow:** Soft shadows (not harsh / studio-style)
- **No:** Hard shadows, extreme contrast, clinical lighting

## 13. SCENE GRAMMAR

Defined separately in `scene-grammar.yaml`. Three core scenes:

### 13.1 SCENE_A: COMPANION (陪伴)

**Context:** Family conversation, household counseling  
**Camera:** Soft mid-shot, warm living-room aesthetic  
**Lighting:** Warm evening light (like a lamp), gentle shadows  
**Background:** Neutral, home-like (soft gray, warm beige)  
**Wardrobe:** LOOK_A (Companion)  
**Gaze:** Listening (steady toward user)  
**Gesture Density:** Low (subtle small nod, slight lean-in)  
**Avatar Distance:** Close, intimate but not invasive  
**Voice Tone:** Conversational, warm, unhurried  

### 13.2 SCENE_B: PRINCIPAL_EXPLAIN (讲解)

**Context:** Teaching moment, explaining family education concepts  
**Camera:** Mid-shot, seated or standing, educational but warm  
**Lighting:** Even, clear (suggests clarity, not spotlit)  
**Background:** Simple, focused (soft gradient or pattern)  
**Wardrobe:** LOOK_B (Principal)  
**Gaze:** Explaining (steady user contact, may shift slightly to illustrate)  
**Gesture Density:** Moderate (small hand gesture to emphasize point)  
**Avatar Distance:** Conversational, clear  
**Voice Tone:** Articulate, thoughtful, paced for understanding  

### 13.3 SCENE_C: TONIGHT_ACTION (今晚)

**Context:** "How to say it tonight" / "One small action" sessions  
**Camera:** Intimate mid-shot, warm and close  
**Lighting:** Warm evening (like candlelight, not harsh)  
**Background:** Intimate, home-like (darker warm tones)  
**Wardrobe:** LOOK_C (Warm Evening)  
**Gaze:** Warm, steady, emotionally present  
**Gesture Density:** Low to moderate (hand on heart, gentle gestures)  
**Avatar Distance:** Very close, supportive  
**Voice Tone:** Intimate, encouraging, slightly lower pace

## 14. PERFORMANCE PLANNER BOUNDARIES

### 14.1 What PerformancePlanner May Decide

- Vocal tone (warm, calm, thoughtful, firm)
- Speech pace (slow for concepts, natural for conversation)
- Pause timing (thinking, emphasis)
- Expression selection from approved set
- Gaze policy selection (listening vs. explaining)
- Gesture selection from approved set
- Scene cue (which of 3 scenes)

### 14.2 What PerformancePlanner CANNOT Decide

❌ **Content mutation** — cannot change what Principal says  
❌ **New expressions** — cannot invent expressions outside the identity map  
❌ **New gestures** — cannot create unlisted motions  
❌ **Family data modification** — cannot alter structured output  
❌ **Safety bypass** — cannot override Human Gate  

**Principle:** **HOW to say** (performance) ≠ **WHAT to say** (content).

## 17. LOCAL 2D AVATAR ARCHITECTURE

### 17.1 Current Layer Structure

```
Canvas2DRenderer
├─ Background / Scene Layer
├─ Character Base Layer
│  ├─ Head (outline + fill)
│  ├─ Hair (silhouette + volume)
│  └─ Neck + shoulders
├─ Expression Layer
│  ├─ Eyes (shape + gaze + blink animation)
│  ├─ Eyebrows
│  └─ Face expression blend
├─ Mouth Layer
│  └─ 8 MouthShapes + viseme mapping
├─ Gesture Layer
│  └─ Hand position + nod / lean-in animation
└─ Gaze + Blink Animation Loop
```

### 17.2 Rendering Contract

- **Single Canvas context**, no external GPU dependencies
- **Vector-based mouth shapes** (quadratic curves, lines)
- **Blink & nod animation** via frame-based phase (0..1)
- **Expression blending** (smooth transition between states)
- **Viseme sync** from SpeechPlaybackClock (no artificial delay)

### 17.3 Test Surface

- **8 mouth shapes render correctly** across all expressions
- **Viseme scheduling** does not drop events
- **Barge-in cancel** does not leave stale audio/mouth in motion
- **Expression transitions** are smooth (no snap/pop)
- **Gaze direction** matches policy (listening = steady)
- **No Principal semantic mutation** from avatar rendering
