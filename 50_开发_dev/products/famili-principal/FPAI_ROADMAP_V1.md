# FPAI Roadmap V1

## Product Destination

`法咪莉校长` is a multimodal digital-human IP for family growth: an interactive AI principal that can eventually teach, converse, guide practice, and appear across text, voice, avatar, classroom, and companion experiences.

The roadmap is staged so the Soul and text intelligence core are proven before presentation layers are activated.

## FP0 - Soul & Corpus Foundation

Status: PASS_INTERNAL.

Outcome: establish product boundary, corpus governance, persona DNA, BTM/FPS distillation rules, response contracts, safety policy, and eval scaffolding. No training or runtime launch.

## FP1 - Prototype Retrieval & Structured Response

Status: AUTHORIZED_TEXT_INTELLIGENCE_MVP.

Scope: reviewed retrieval over approved method/knowledge cards, structured response generation behind replaceable model gateway, contract validation, safety routing, and eval.

## FP2 - Companion Workflow

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Possible future scope: 21-day companion, longitudinal check-ins, and outcome capture. This cannot begin without FP1 gate, consent design, and Human Gate policy.

## FP3 - Voice / Avatar / Digital Human

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Future scope: define the public-facing multimodal IP expression: voice direction, avatar design, teaching presence, classroom-style delivery, and digital-human production.

Voice cloning, likeness cloning, lip sync, real-time avatar, and digital-human production are outside FP1 and require explicit rights, consent, safety, and owner approval.
