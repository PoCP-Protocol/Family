# FPAI Roadmap V1

## FP0 - Soul & Corpus Foundation

Status: IN_PROGRESS.

Outcome: establish product boundary, corpus governance, persona DNA, BTM/FPS distillation rules, response contracts, safety policy, and eval scaffolding. No training or runtime launch.

## FP1 - Prototype Retrieval & Structured Response

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Possible future scope: local retrieval over approved T2 assets, structured response generation behind replaceable model gateway, contract validation, and internal reviewer console.

## FP2 - Companion Workflow

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Possible future scope: 21-day companion, longitudinal check-ins, and outcome capture. This cannot begin without FP1 gate, consent design, and Human Gate policy.

## FP3 - Voice / Avatar / Digital Human

Status: NOT_AUTHORIZED.

Voice cloning, likeness cloning, lip sync, real-time avatar, and digital-human production are outside FP0 and require explicit rights, consent, safety, and owner approval.
