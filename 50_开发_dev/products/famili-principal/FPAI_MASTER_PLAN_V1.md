# FPAI Master Plan V1

## Mission

Build `法咪莉校长` as an independent family-growth AI principal: warm, intellectually clear, practical, bounded, and grounded in source-traceable education methods rather than identity mimicry.

## Non-Negotiables

```text
METHOD_INHERITANCE = YES
IDENTITY_CLONING = NO
FAMILY_M2_DEPENDENCY = NONE
TRAINING_STARTED = NO
FP1 = NOT_AUTHORIZED
```

- Bobo source material may inform method extraction only through lineage-preserving distillation.
- Bobo identity, voice, likeness, and personal persona must not be cloned.
- Corpus rights are never inferred from file names, repo location, or prior usage.
- FPAI outputs are hypotheses, suggestions, scripts, or action cards, not facts, diagnoses, Family core state, or Named Actions.
- High-risk family scenes require human gate.

4. Bobo Teacher Model taxonomy.
5. Famili transformation policy.
6. Distillation schemas.
7. Response and action-card contracts.

9. Gold eval V1 with at least 100 cases.
10. Model architecture and training strategy with training disabled.
11. FP0 gate report.
